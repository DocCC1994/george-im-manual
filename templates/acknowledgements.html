How this edition was put together

Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, was used as a writing and editing tool. It gathered the previous George Hospital protocols, the South African STG/EML and the international guidelines into one structure; drafted and condensed text and tables in the house style; and marked every place where sources disagree (DECISION) or a fact needs local confirmation (CHECK). It made no clinical decisions.

Every source it used and every page it drafted was read, corrected and approved by the clinicians named in the footer of that page, and the book cannot be released while any marker remains open.
