How the pages work

Part of the pageWhat it does
Acute HandbookWhat a doctor needs in the first hours: one or two pages per topic, built on fixed rows (RECOGNISE, INVESTIGATE, IMMEDIATE, ONGOING, REFER) with side boxes.
Reference ManualThe fuller picture for the ward and clinic: diagnosis, treatment choices, where the guidelines differ, and the sources. Each claim links to its source.
Running header and title bandThe book and system sit at the top of every page; the topic, version, effective date and review date sit in the grey band.
FooterThe author, version, build date, STG/EML edition and approval line of every page, and Suggest an edit, which emails the editors with the topic already in the subject line.
LinksUnderlined text is a link. Back to contents is at the top right of each page; citations such as [STG 2024] open the source.

Order of authoritySouth African STG/EML first, then the relevant international guideline (usually ESC), then the previous GRH protocol. Where they differ, the Reference Manual has a Where the guidelines differ table with George's decision.
