RECOGNISE

Hyperkalaemia = K+ > 6.0 mmol/L needing emergency measures [STG]. UKKA grades it mild 5.5–5.9, moderate 6.0–6.4, severe ≥ 6.5 [UKKA] DECISION: start emergency treatment at K+ > 6.0 (STG) or ≥ 6.5 without ECG changes (UKKA)

Often silent until arrest. Weakness, ascending paralysis, palpitations, bradycardia. ECG: peaked T → small or absent P, long PR → broad QRS → sine wave, VF or asystole. A normal ECG does not exclude danger.

Pseudohyperkalaemia: haemolysed or delayed sample, very high WCC or platelets. Repeat on a blood gas, but never delay treatment if the ECG is abnormal.

Causes: AKI or CKD · drugs: ACEi, ARB, MRA (spironolactone), NSAIDs, high-dose co-trimoxazole (PJP), heparin, BB · DKA · adrenal crisis · rhabdomyolysis, haemolysis, tumour lysis · digoxin toxicity · K+ in IV fluids or supplements.

INVESTIGATE

Now: 12-lead ECG and cardiac monitor · K+ on a blood gas (with pH, HCO3, glucose) · U&E, creatinine, Mg, Ca, phosphate · FBC · urine output.

By cause: CK (rhabdomyolysis) · ketones (DKA) · uric acid, LDH (tumour lysis) · cortisol if adrenal crisis is suspected · digoxin level (takes days; do not wait for it).

IMMEDIATE

K+ > 6.0 or any ECG change: all three steps, in this order [STG]

  1. Protect the heart: calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL slow IV over 10 min. Repeat if ECG changes persist after 5 min or recur. Works in minutes and lasts about 30–60 min; it does not lower K+. UKKA gives 30 mL DECISION: 10 mL (STG) or 30 mL (UKKA) as the first dose
  2. Shift K+ into cells: dextrose 50% 100 mL IV, then soluble insulin 10 units IV as a push over 5 min. Works within about 30 min and lasts 4–6 hrs CHECK onset and duration (UKKA). Glucose hourly for 6 hrs [STG]. UKKA: 25 g glucose (50 mL of 50%), plus a dextrose 10% infusion for 5 hrs if the glucose before treatment is < 7 mmol/L DECISION: dextrose 100 mL (STG) or 50 mL plus infusion (UKKA) CHECK UKKA figures against the PDF
  3. Add a β2-agonist (salbutamol) 10–20 mg by nebuliser [STG], with insulin, never instead of it. Caution in IHD and tachyarrhythmia.

Then remove K+: stop K+ in fluids, supplements and culprit drugs; treat the cause; volume-deplete AKI: sodium chloride 0.9%. Dialysis, or if not feasible potassium binder (sodium polystyrene sulfonate) 15 g orally with 15 mL lactulose 6hrly, or 30–60 g rectally as an enema, washed out after 8 hrs with a phosphate enema (less effective) [STG].

Sodium bicarbonate: STG silent; not routine. Severe metabolic acidosis only, and never in the line used for calcium DECISION: role and dose of bicarbonate (UKKA: in acidosis with CKD)

ONGOING

Cardiac monitor until K+ < 6.0 and the ECG is normal. Repeat K+ at 1, 2, 4 and 6 hrs CHECK UKKA timing; insulin wears off at 4–6 hrs: rebound is common. K+ still > 6.0: repeat insulin-dextrose. Watch for late hypoglycaemia, worse in AKI.

Review every drug (stop ACEi, ARB, MRA, NSAIDs, trimethoprim for now); low-K+ diet advice; plan when to restart.

REFER

Discuss with the IM consultant now for dialysis: K+ > 7 refractory to medical therapy, pH < 7.15, refractory pulmonary oedema, uraemic complications [STG]; also K+ ≥ 6.5 with ECG changes, oliguric AKI, or rising K+ despite treatment. HIV is not a contraindication to acute dialysis [STG]. Arrest: ALS, with calcium and insulin-dextrose during CPR.

Dose box CHECK

Calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV over 10 min; repeat after 5 min if the ECG has not improved [STG].

Dextrose 50% 100 mL IV, then soluble insulin (Actrapid) 10 units IV push over 5 min. Glucose hourly for 6 hrs [STG].

Salbutamol 10–20 mg nebulised: 0.5% solution (5 mg/mL), 1 mL (5 mg) made up to 4 mL with sodium chloride 0.9% per nebule, back to back [STG].

Sodium polystyrene sulfonate 15 g orally with lactulose 15 mL 6hrly; or 30–60 g rectally.

Avoid
  • Waiting for a repeat lab K+ when the ECG is abnormal
  • Insulin without dextrose
  • Calcium and bicarbonate in the same line (precipitates)
  • K+-containing fluids and supplements
  • Suxamethonium if intubating
Mitigation · dialysis delayed
  • Repeat insulin-dextrose every 4–6 hrs as K+ rebounds; salbutamol between doses.
  • Binder 6hrly; loop diuretic (furosemide) if passing urine and not dry CHECK dose.
  • Monitor in EC resus until dialysis starts.
