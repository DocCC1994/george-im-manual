Open questions for the section owner

  1. Where are DKA and HHS managed at George (HOCU, ward, ICU), and does that depend on severity?
  2. One standard insulin concentration: 1 unit/mL syringe driver (GRH) or 0.25 unit/mL bag (STG)?
  3. Saline or Ringer's lactate as the default fluid?
  4. Once glucose is < 14: reduce insulin to 0.05 units/kg/hr (consensus) or 1–2 units/hr (STG)?
  5. Potassium: confirm the 20 mmol/hr peripheral limit. Are premixed potassium bags available?
  6. Bicarbonate: never (STG), or consider at pH < 7.0 after consultant discussion?
  7. HHS: 0.45% saline by corrected sodium (STG) or only when osmolality stalls (consensus)? Full-dose enoxaparin or prophylaxis?
  8. Continue the patient's isophane during the infusion?
  9. Are bedside ketone meters available, and where? Who supervises the infusion overnight?
