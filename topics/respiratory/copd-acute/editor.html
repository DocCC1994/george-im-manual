Open questions for the section owner

  1. NIV threshold: BTS (pH < 7.35 and PaCO2 > 6.5 after 1 hour of treatment) or GRH 2016 and GOLD (pH ≤ 7.35 and/or PaCO2 ≥ 6.0 at once)?
  2. Where may NIV run at George (HOCU only, or a ward with 1:1 nursing)? How many machines, and who sets them up at night?
  3. Nebulisers: air-driven (GOLD) or oxygen-driven at 6–8 L/min (STG)? Is piped air or a compressor available in casualty and the wards?
  4. SABA dosing in severe exacerbations: continuous for the first hour (STG) or hourly for 2–3 doses (GOLD)?
  5. Antibiotic route on admission: amoxicillin/clavulanate 1.2 g IV 8hrly for every admitted patient (SAASP 2026), or oral 875/125 mg 12hrly with IV only if unable to swallow (STG)? Use the SAASP CRP < 40 mg/L rule to withhold antibiotics when only one cardinal symptom is present?
  6. Maintenance LABA/ICS: budesonide/formoterol (GRH 2024) or salmeterol/fluticasone (STG)? Is formoterol still on code?
  7. HFNT: available at George, and for whom?
  8. ABG on room air (GRH 2016) or on controlled oxygen?
  9. Pulmonary rehabilitation: is there a physiotherapy programme to refer to?
