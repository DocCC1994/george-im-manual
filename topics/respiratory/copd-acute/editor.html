Open questions for the section owner

  1. NIV threshold: BTS (pH < 7.35 and PaCO2 > 6.5 after 1 hour of treatment), the George SOP 2024 (RR > 30, pH < 7.35, PaCO2 > 6.7, PaO2 < 6.7 kPa on room air, not responding), or GRH 2016 and GOLD (pH ≤ 7.35 and/or PaCO2 ≥ 6.0 at once)? The SOP also limits candidacy (ECOG 1–2, mMRC 1–3, no home oxygen, first EC presentation in 12 months).
  2. Ward NIV: the questionnaire (S2) allows starts in the ward or EC, and the SOP asks for intensive nursing with continuous SpO2 and cardiac monitoring. What nursing ratio and doctor cover? How many machines, and who sets them up at night?
  3. Nebulisers: air-driven (GOLD) or oxygen-driven at 6–8 L/min (STG)? Is piped air or a compressor available in casualty and the wards?
  4. SABA dosing in severe exacerbations: continuous for the first hour (STG) or hourly for 2–3 doses (GOLD)?
  5. Antibiotic route on admission: amoxicillin/clavulanate 1.2 g IV 8hrly for every admitted patient (SAASP 2026), or oral 875/125 mg 12hrly with IV only if unable to swallow (STG)? George follows SAASP (questionnaire D4). Use the SAASP CRP < 40 mg/L rule to withhold antibiotics when only one cardinal symptom is present?
  6. Maintenance LABA/ICS: budesonide/formoterol (GRH 2024) or salmeterol/fluticasone (STG)? Is formoterol still on code?
  7. HFNO is on the wards, EC, resus and ICU (S2). Use it only when NIV is not tolerated (draft), or first for hypoxaemic failure (GOLD 2026, George SOP)?
  8. ABG on room air (GRH 2016) or on controlled oxygen?
  9. Pulmonary rehabilitation: is there a physiotherapy programme to refer to? Tertiary pulmonology centre for referral?
