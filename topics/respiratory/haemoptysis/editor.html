Basis of this draft

New topic: there is no GRH haemoptysis protocol in the Unit protocols or GEORGE IM Handbook folders, and the STG 2024 has no haemoptysis section (it only says to refer major haemoptysis under bronchiectasis §16.3 and lung abscess §16.5). The draft is built on the Western Cape documents (Metro West/GSH major haemoptysis protocol 2009, Shaw "Approach to haemoptysis" 2020, Paarl Hospital guidelines 2025), checked against UpToDate (three topics, 2025–26) and three SA papers (Gross 2009 and Shaw 2023 from Tygerberg, Alexander 2013 from Durban). No ESC or ERS guideline on haemoptysis exists. Antibiotics follow STG §16.3 and SAASP 2026 (p. 33); warfarin reversal follows STG §19.19.

Open questions for the section owner

  1. Volume threshold for life-threatening haemoptysis: UpToDate (≥ 150 mL/24 hrs or ≥ 100 mL/hr), Metro West (> 250 mL), or a clinical definition only?
  2. Tranexamic acid: systemic 1 g 8hrly (Shaw 2020), nebulised 500 mg 8hrly (UpToDate, one small RCT), or none?
  3. Cough suppression on the ward: oral morphine (Paarl) or codeine 30 mg 6hrly (Metro West)?
  4. Hb target while bleeding: ≥ 10 g/dL (Paarl) or a restrictive target?
  5. CT angiography at George before transfer, or transfer first? Local facts: contrast CT is mostly daytime, after hours only if it cannot wait and with a doctor accompanying (I1); there is no interventional radiology at George (I11). Does George radiology have a haemoptysis CTA protocol (Tygerberg uses a single-phase CTPA with bolus tracking)?
  6. IV antibiotic for the unwell patient: ceftriaxone (STG §16.3) or amoxicillin/clavulanate 1.2 g IV 8hrly (SAASP CAP/lung abscess)? Oral dose: 875/125 mg (STG) or 1 g (SAASP)?
  7. Start TB treatment before the TB-NAAT result in life-threatening bleeding with a typical CXR?
  8. Referral centre and route for embolisation and pulmonary artery plugging (Tygerberg?). Bronchoscopy at George is now answered from the local facts (S7).
  9. Aspergillus serology and ANCA through NHLS: available, and turnaround?

The uploaded "Management of Tuberculosis" guideline is the 2024 children and adolescents guideline, so it was not used for this adult topic. Nebulised tranexamic acid dose: UpToDate gives 500 mg/5 mL for up to five days; the 8hrly frequency is from the trial as commonly cited, not from the text read (CHECK).
