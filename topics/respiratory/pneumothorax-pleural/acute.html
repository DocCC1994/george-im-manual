RECOGNISE

Sudden pleuritic chest pain and breathlessness; reduced breath sounds, hyper-resonance on the affected side.

Tension pneumothorax: hypoxia, tachycardia, hypotension, distended neck veins, tracheal shift (late); in a ventilated patient, rising airway pressures and sudden desaturation. A clinical diagnosis: decompress before the CXR. One of the causes of obstructive shock and PEA arrest [STG].

INVESTIGATE

Erect CXR. Size: a rim ≥ 2 cm at the level of the hilum is "large" CHECK BTS 2023 size rule. POCUS (EC, ICU): no lung sliding, lung point. CT (daytime) only if unclear: bulla against pneumothorax, before surgery. Blood gas in SSP. HIV test; sputum Xpert if TB is possible.

Tension: oxygen 15 L/min; needle decompression with a 14–16G cannula (4th or 5th intercostal space, anterior to mid-axillary line, or 2nd space mid-clavicular), then an intercostal drain (ICD) at once CHECK site (BTS 2023, ATLS).

Procedure box CHECK

Needle aspiration: 16–18G cannula, 2nd intercostal space mid-clavicular or the safe triangle; 3-way tap and 50 mL syringe; stop at 2.5 L, if resistance, or with coughing.

Small-bore ICD (Seldinger, 12–14F) for air and for free-flowing fluid; large-bore for blood, and often for thick pus.

Safe triangle: lateral edge of pectoralis major, anterior edge of latissimus dorsi, the 5th intercostal space; apex below the axilla. Above the rib.

RECOGNISE

Large effusion with distress (dyspnoea, hypoxia, mediastinal shift) · pleural infection: fever or slow response in pneumonia with an effusion (→ CAP) · haemothorax (trauma, anticoagulation, procedure). In SA most exudates are TB, then parapneumonic and malignant. Bilateral with a big heart: think heart failure.

TAP FIRST

Aspirate and analyse every new effusion [STG], ultrasound guided where possible. GRH: no tap for clear heart failure without atypical features (unequal bilateral, pleurisy, fever, no cardiomegaly, not resolving), or for a rim < 1 cm [GRH 2016].

Send: appearance · pH (heparinised blood gas syringe; not if frank pus) · protein, LDH, glucose with a serum protein and LDH · cell count and differential · Gram stain and MCS · Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra and TB culture · ADA CHECK NHLS ADA availability · cytology if malignancy is possible.

Exudate (Light): fluid/serum protein > 0.5, fluid/serum LDH > 0.6, or fluid LDH > ⅔ of the upper limit of serum LDH CHECK.

Drainage rules
  • Therapeutic tap: max 1–1.5 L at a time (STG TB: 1 L); stop for cough, chest tightness or a vasovagal response CHECK
  • ICD for fluid: drain max 1.5 L in the first hour, then clamp and release slowly (re-expansion pulmonary oedema) CHECK BTS
  • Check platelets and INR when anticoagulated or in liver disease; do not delay an emergency drain for them.
  • Never clamp a drain for air.
