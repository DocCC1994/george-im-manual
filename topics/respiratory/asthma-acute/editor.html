Settled by Craig (21 Oct 2026)

  • Severity: GRH 2016/BTS grades. Prednisone 5–14 days case by case. Magnesium 2 g in 100 mL over 20 min, added to recipes.yaml.
  • No aminophylline or theophylline; in-line salbutamol when ventilated. The IM team on call (MO and consultant) plans ventilation; there is no ICU consultant.
  • Discharge inhaler: budesonide/formoterol (160/4.5 and 320/9 on code). No PI-based ART at George, so the STG beclomethasone note is dropped.
  • PEF meters are in ICU.
