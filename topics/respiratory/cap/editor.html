Basis of this draft

Built on the GRH community-acquired pneumonia guideline 2024 (Revision 3, effective 05/2024, Dr A Vermeulen, Dr J P Spanos). Checked against STG 2024 §16.6 and §16.7 (doses; §10.2.9 for PJP), SAASP 2026 Ch 7 pp. 36–37 (antibiotic choice and duration), the ERS/ESICM/ESCMID/ALAT severe CAP guideline 2023 (uploaded) and the George SOP on ventilation and ICU prioritisation 2024. Tier 2 topic: acute sheet only; the reference chapter is deferred. NICE NG250 was offered as optional and not uploaded. The Calgary "Cough, Dyspnea & Fever" approach was not needed for a two-page acute sheet.

Open questions for Craig

  1. Severe CAP: CURB-65 ≥ 3 alone (GRH) or any one STG feature (cyanosis, confusion, hypotension, RR > 30)?
  2. Non-severe CAP with age ≥ 65 or comorbidity: ampicillin (GRH) or ceftriaxone (STG; SAASP also allows amoxicillin-clavulanate IV)?
  3. CURB-65 2: add azithromycin, or keep it for severe CAP and suspected atypical pneumonia?
  4. Sputum MCS in every admission (GRH) or only in severe CAP and major comorbidity (SAASP)?
  5. Steroids in hypoxic severe CAP without shock (GRH, CAPE COD-style criterion) or only with shock (ERS/ESICM 2023)? Which agent and dose?
  6. NIV in severe CAP (GRH 2024) or HFNO only (George SOP)?
  7. Aspiration: amoxicillin-clavulanate (STG §16.7) or standard CAP regimen (ERS/ESICM 2023)?
  8. Oral switch criteria: STG, SAASP or GRH?
  9. Follow-up CXR: routine at 4–6 weeks if > 50 (STG) or only with persistent symptoms (GRH)?
  10. Admitting team: GRH says Family Medicine for CURB-65 ≤ 2 and IM for > 3. Who admits CURB-65 3?
  11. Oseltamivir: stocked at George? Who authorises? (Not on the STG/EML, so the GRH dose stands until checked; renal dose adjustment needed.)
  12. Legionella urine antigen and respiratory viral PCR: available through NHLS George, and after hours?
  13. Ceftriaxone IV administration and the azithromycin dilution (500 mg in 250 mL over 60 min, from the package insert): match ward practice?
