Settled by Craig (21 Oct 2026)

  • SAASP antibiotic recommendations take preference. Severe CAP = any one of cyanosis, confusion, hypotension, RR > 30, or CURB-65 ≥ 3 (SAASP). Oral switch on SAASP criteria (HR < 100, RR < 25 on room air, no delirium, able to take oral).
  • Not severe, < 65, no comorbidity: amoxicillin or doxycycline orally; ampicillin IV only if not tolerating oral. Not severe, ≥ 65 or comorbidity: ceftriaxone (not ampicillin). CURB-65 2: azithromycin only if severe or atypical suspected. Aspiration: amoxicillin-clavulanate.
  • Updated GRH protocol (2024, Rev 3, Craig's copy): sputum MCS in every admission (GRH practice, unlike SAASP); blood cultures also when sepsis is suspected; azithromycin IV only if unable to swallow and no NG tube; no hydrocortisone in proven or suspected influenza; viral swabs to Pathcare on the NHLS viral swab.
  • Moxifloxacin is not really an option at George: doxycycline for penicillin allergy; moxifloxacin availability marked CHECK. No induced sputum for PJP PCR; HIV-positive patients get sputum Xpert and TB culture. CALL row goes to the IM doctor on call.
