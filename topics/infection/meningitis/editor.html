Open questions for the section owner

  1. Dexamethasone in bacterial meningitis: STG says no (no benefit in low- and middle-income trials, mostly HIV-positive patients presenting late); ESCMID gives 10 mg 6hrly for 4 days to all, stopping if not pneumococcal or H. influenzae. Options: none (STG); all suspected bacterial meningitis (ESCMID); or only HIV negative (or HIV on suppressive ART) with a turbid CSF or Gram-positive diplococci, and never if CrAg positive. The owner's brief asked for "ceftriaxone + dexamethasone"; the draft shows the ESCMID dose with this DECISION.
  2. CT before LP: STG "reduced level of consciousness" or SAASP's GCS ≤ 10? Add advanced HIV (CD4 < 100) as an indication, given toxoplasmosis and TB mass lesions?
  3. Empiric Listeria cover (ampicillin for age > 50, immunosuppression, alcohol, cancer, pregnancy): adopt SAASP, or keep STG (ceftriaxone only, ampicillin once Listeria grows)?
  4. TBM steroids in HIV: give (as many SA units do), withhold (ACT HIV), or decide by grade?
  5. Communicating hydrocephalus in TBM: serial LPs at George, or always discuss with GSH? Acetazolamide is not stocked.
  6. Crypto induction at George: confirm the default when flucytosine is out of stock (deoxycholate + fluconazole 1 200 mg for 14 days), and who submits the liposomal amphotericin application and how (local facts M8: name of the approval body to confirm).
  7. Amphotericin pre-hydration: GRH 2021 (Ringer's lactate + KCl 20 mmol + MgSO4 2 g over 2 h, plus oral K and Mg 12hrly) or STG (sodium chloride 0.9%)? Oral potassium and magnesium doses to state.
  8. Stopping maintenance fluconazole: STG (≥ 1 year, CD4 > 200) or GRH 2021 (also suppressed viral load)?
  9. Therapeutic LP: the trigger (opening pressure ≥ 25 cmH2O, or symptoms only as in the STG); are manometers available in EC and the wards?
  10. Monitoring on amphotericin: every 3 days (GRH) or twice weekly (STG)?
  11. Local: which CSF tests run after hours (L11); CSF volume the lab needs for Xpert Ultra; notification route and category for meningococcal disease.
