Open questions for the section owner

  1. First vasopressor: adrenaline (STG, EML) or noradrenaline (SSC, strong)? George stocks no noradrenaline, which matches the STG (local facts S3).
  2. Peripheral vasopressor: George already allows low-dose adrenaline via a large-bore cannula, central line as doses rise (S3), in line with SSC. Confirm the dose at which a central line is needed and the site checks.
  3. Fluid: sodium chloride 0.9% first (STG 20.12) or Ringer's lactate first (STG 23.10, SSC)?
  4. Antibiotic timing: adopt SSC's 3-hour window for possible sepsis without shock, or keep 1 hour for everyone (STG)?
  5. Blood cultures: two sets (SAASP, SSC, UpToDate) or one (cost)?
  6. Hospital-acquired sepsis: what is the George first choice? George has no local antibiogram and uses SAASP (D4), so the SAASP Groote Schuur example is the default. Piperacillin-tazobactam + amikacin, ertapenem, or meropenem?
  7. Community septic shock: add a single aminoglycoside dose to ceftriaxone (UpToDate suggests two classes for Gram-negative shock), or ceftriaxone alone (STG)?
  8. Prolonged β-lactam infusion (SSC strong): use on the ward, or ICU only? Pumps and lines available?
  9. MAP 60–65 for patients ≥ 65 years (new in SSC 2026): adopt?
  10. Steroids: keep STG's threshold (adrenaline ≥ 0.25 mcg/kg/min for > 4 h) or start earlier in any septic shock (SSC, no threshold)?
  11. Escalation: where a patient on adrenaline is nursed when ICU is full (no medical HOCU known). Which tertiary ICU receives George patients (decided on telemedicine rounds; centre not named).
  12. Local: is there an EC sepsis pack; which screening tool is on the charts; aminoglycoside and vancomycin level turnaround.
