Open questions for the section owner

  1. Severe anaemia cut-off: Hb < 7 g/dL (NDoH/WHO, GRH 2019) or Hb < 6 g/dL (STG 2024), both for "severe" and for transfusion?
  2. Hyperparasitaemia: > 4% (NDoH/WHO) or 5% (STG, GRH)? Admit and give IV artesunate at 2–4% (NDoH)?
  3. GRH extra criteria: keep ALT > 3 × ULN and "any raised creatinine" as severe features (GRH), or use the NDoH creatinine > 265 µmol/L?
  4. Antibiotic in severe malaria: who gets it (shock, HIV, pneumonia, neutrophilia, deterioration?) and ceftriaxone 1 g (GRH) or 2 g (STG sepsis dose)?
  5. Quinine fallback: keep it (NDoH, GRH) although the STG lists only artesunate? If kept, should a weight-banded quinine recipe go into recipes.yaml (500 mL over 4 h)?
  6. Vasopressor in severe malaria: NDoH lists adrenaline among harmful adjuncts in cerebral malaria, but George has only adrenaline. Accept adrenaline for shock (MAP < 65) with that caution?
  7. Artesunate: is IV artesunate in stock at George, where, and is the Section 21 authorisation current (local facts M7)? The 2017 approval was for 20 vials of emergency stock and needs renewal every 6 months.
  8. Follow-up FBC: add day 14 FBC after hyperparasitaemia (post-artesunate delayed haemolysis), on top of the GRH day 10 FBC and smear?
  9. Tick bite fever, no oral route: doxycycline crushed by nasogastric tube (common practice, not in the STG) before IV ciprofloxacin? Is IV chloramphenicol available?
  10. VHF: which room at George is used for a suspected VHF patient, where the PPE kit is kept, who the infection control contact is after hours, and which Western Cape centre is the designated VHF referral centre? Should the NICD numbers be printed here or wait for the who-to-call block?
  11. Ribavirin for CCHF: mention it only as "on NICD or ID advice" (current draft), or give the dose table?
