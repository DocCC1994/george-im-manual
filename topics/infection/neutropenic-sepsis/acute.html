RECOGNISE

Febrile neutropenia = neutrophils < 0.5 × 109/L and temperature > 38 °C for > 1 hr, or a single 38.3 °C [STG]. Any neutropenic patient who looks septic counts, with or without fever (steroids, elderly). ESMO also counts neutrophils expected to fall below 0.5 within 48 hrs (after chemotherapy) [ESMO] DECISION: include expected neutropenia

A medical emergency: a minor infection can progress to septic shock within hours [STG]. Signs of infection are muted (little pus, no infiltrate on CXR).

Who: chemotherapy in the last 6 weeks CHECK window, haematological malignancy, marrow infiltration (TB, lymphoma), drugs (carbimazole, clozapine, co-trimoxazole, penicillins, carbamazepine, valproate) [STG], HIV and its drugs, B12 or folate deficiency. Cause unclear → Severe neutropenia, Pancytopenia.

INVESTIGATE

Before the first dose, without delaying it [STG]: blood cultures (2 sets: peripheral, plus every lumen of a central line) · FBC with differential, U&E, LFT, CRP · venous gas with lactate · urine dipstick and MCS · CXR · sputum MCS (and Xpert if TB is possible) · swab of any skin, line or mouth lesion · malaria test after travel · HIV test if status unknown. No rectal examination or rectal temperatures.

FIRST HOUR
  1. Empiric antibiotics within 1 hr of arrival or of the first fever on the ward [ESMO] (STG: promptly) CHECK 1-hr target in ESMO.
  2. Resuscitate as for sepsis: fluid, lactate, vasopressor for shock → Sepsis.
  3. Stop the culprit drug; consider removing a central line [STG].
  4. Isolate (side room): reverse barrier care, with the droplet precautions of SAASP [SAASP].

REFER

Every case: discuss with the haematologist or oncologist [STG] (on site at George). Call the IM consultant for shock, lactate ≥ 4, respiratory failure or falling GCS; ICU by the George SOP → ICU referral. Low-risk outpatient treatment (MASCC score) is not used here DECISION: admit all (ESMO allows low-risk oral treatment)

Avoid
  • Waiting for the FBC result before the first dose when neutropenia is likely
  • Rectal examination, suppositories, enemas
  • Ertapenem
  • Stopping antibiotics while still neutropenic and only just afebrile
Mitigation · when something is missing
  • No blood culture bottles or line access: give the first dose; culture as soon as possible.
