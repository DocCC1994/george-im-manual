RECOGNISE

Amitriptyline and other tricyclics: toxic from 10–20 mg/kg [GRH 2021] (about 28 tablets of 25 mg in a 70-kg adult). Patients can deteriorate fast, even when they look well on arrival [STG].

Mild to moderateSevere
Drowsiness; anticholinergic: tachycardia, dilated pupils, dry mouth, delirium, urinary retention, absent bowel soundsComa, seizures, hypotension, broad QRS, ventricular arrhythmias, pulmonary oedema

ECG (the key test) [GRH 2021]: sinus tachycardia (commonest) · QRS > 100 ms · R in aVR > 3 mm or R/S in aVR > 0.7 · deep slurred S in I and aVL · long PR and QTc · VT or VF.

INVESTIGATE

12-lead ECG now, then hourly for 6 hrs or until normal; continuous monitor. Blood gas (pH), U&E with K+ and Na+, glucose, paracetamol level at 4 hrs, pregnancy test. TCA levels do not help [GRH 2021].

FIRST HOURS
  1. Airway: intubate early if GCS ≤ 8, seizing or deteriorating [STG]. Once ventilated, aim for pH 7.45–7.55: bicarbonate plus a raised minute ventilation (hyperventilation alone if bicarbonate is short or fluids are limited).
  2. Sodium bicarbonate for QRS > 100 ms, ventricular arrhythmia, hypotension not responding to fluid, or seizures [STG]: bolus, repeat until the QRS narrows, then the infusion (dose box). Discuss with the IM consultant.
  3. Hypotension: sodium chloride 0.9% bolus (GRH 2021: 1–2 L), but watch for pulmonary oedema; the cause is myocardial depression and vasodilation [STG]. MAP still < 65: bicarbonate, then adrenaline infusion (dose box) → ICU.
  4. Seizures or agitation: benzodiazepine (dose box) → Status epilepticus. No phenytoin; no flumazenil in a mixed or unknown overdose [STG].
  5. Activated charcoal 50 g once the airway is safe or protected; can help after 1 hr because gastric emptying is slow [STG] DECISION: STG beyond 1 hr; GRH 2021 within 1 hr. No gastric lavage [GRH 2021] DECISION: GRH contraindicates; STG allows within 1 hr if life-threatening and airway protected.
REFRACTORY

Rare once bicarbonate is running; rethink the diagnosis [GRH 2021]. Unstable VT: synchronised cardioversion. Refractory arrhythmia: magnesium sulfate. Impending arrest: lipid emulsion CHECK availability → Arrhythmias.

MONITOR

On bicarbonate: blood gas with Na+ and K+ hourly until the pH is stable at target, then 4hrly; accept Na+ up to 155 [GRH 2021]. Continuous ECG monitor; 12-lead after each change. QRS widens again: bolus and increase the infusion. Stable (pH 7.5, QRS normal, BP stable): cut the infusion rate by a quarter every 4 hrs.

DISPOSITION
AND CALL

Discuss with the IM registrar or consultant on call: every patient with an abnormal ECG, hypotension, seizures, GCS ≤ 8 or on bicarbonate; all intubated patients [GRH 2021] → ICU.

Medically fit when no symptoms, haemodynamics stable and ECGs normal at 6 hrs [GRH 2021] DECISION: GRH 6 hrs; STG asymptomatic, or mild signs with an ECG normal for at least 24 hrs. Self-harm: psychiatry and social work once medically stable.
