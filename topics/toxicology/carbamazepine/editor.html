Basis of this draft

The manifest lists an undated GRH carbamazepine protocol, but none was found on Drive (searched by title and full text). The STG has no carbamazepine or phenytoin section: it gives charcoal (Table 19.2, multiple doses for carbamazepine) and "no phenytoin in cardiotoxic poisoning". The rest is international practice (EXTRIP 2014 carbamazepine, EXTRIP 2016 phenytoin), marked DECISION. Local facts rev 109: L7 levels, M10 antiepileptics, S5 haemodialysis. Acute Handbook only.
