RECOGNISE

Weed killer (blue-green liquid with a stench and an emetic added). Burning mouth and throat, vomiting, diarrhoea; mouth and tongue ulcers appear over hours. Kidney and liver failure in 1–3 days, then lung fibrosis over 1–3 weeks [STG 2024 §19.18.3]. A mouthful of concentrate (more than about 10–20 mL of 20%) is usually fatal CHECK: figure not in STG. Skin contact is rarely toxic unless the skin is broken.
