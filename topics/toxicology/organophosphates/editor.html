Basis of this draft

Rewrite of the GRH organophosphate toxicity guideline 2016 (Rev 1, Dr TF Roos, Dr T Gould; GEORGE IM Handbook folder, 2022 PDF), checked against STG 2024 §19.1, §19.18.1 (amitraz) and §19.18.2. Acute Handbook only, one page (toxicology format, Craig 22 Oct 2026). No guideline PDFs requested: no European guideline covers OP poisoning; STG is the dose source.
