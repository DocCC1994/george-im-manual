RECOGNISE

Often no symptoms for 24 hrs (nausea, vomiting at most), so the history and the level decide. Liver injury from 24 hrs (RUQ pain, ALT up), peak at 72–96 hrs: INR up, hypoglycaemia, encephalopathy, acidosis, AKI [STG].

Toxic dose: > 200 mg/kg or 10 g, whichever is less, in 24 hrs [STG] DECISION: STG 200 mg/kg or 10 g; GRH 2021 > 7.5 g. Repeated supratherapeutic ingestion (RSTI): > 200 mg/kg or 10 g in 24 hrs, > 300 mg/kg or 12 g in 48 hrs, or > 60 mg/kg/day for > 48 hrs with symptoms.

High risk (toxic at lower doses): alcohol misuse, chronic liver disease, enzyme inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin, efavirenz), malnutrition, AIDS, fasting or recent illness.

INVESTIGATE

Paracetamol level at 4 hrs after ingestion or later, never earlier; note the time on the form. ALT, INR, U&E, glucose. Unwell, late or high ALT: blood gas with lactate. Pregnancy test. Check for co-ingestants: ECG for TCA or other cardiotoxins → General approach.

FIRST HOURS
  1. ABC and glucose. Drowsy patients: suspect a co-ingestant; paracetamol alone does not cause early coma.
  2. Activated charcoal 50 g if within 1–2 hrs of a toxic dose and the airway is safe [STG] DECISION: STG 1–2 hrs; GRH 2021 up to 4 hrs.
  3. Start acetylcysteine (NAC) by time since ingestion [STG]:

MONITOR
AND STOP

Near the end of bag 2: ALT, INR and paracetamol level. Continue NAC (bag 2 rate, repeat bags) if the level is still measurable, ALT is raised, or either is abnormal > 24 hrs after ingestion [STG]. Then ALT, INR, creatinine, glucose and gas daily; glucose 6hrly.

Stop when the level is undetectable and ALT is falling: below 500 or falling [EC sheet] DECISION: EC sheet (ALT < 500 or falling) or GRH 2021 (ALT normal or clearly falling, and INR < 1.5). No repeat levels once NAC has stopped.

DISPOSITION

Level below the line with normal ALT: medically fit. Self-harm: psychiatry and social work once medically stable, usually from the ward.

CALL

Discuss with the IM registrar or consultant on call: NAC needed beyond the two bags · INR or bilirubin rising [GRH 2021] · encephalopathy · pH < 7.30 or lactate rising after fluids · creatinine rising · hypoglycaemia. They decide on the liver unit (acute liver failure → Acute liver failure).
