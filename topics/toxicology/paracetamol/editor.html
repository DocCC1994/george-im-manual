Basis of this draft

Built on the GRH paracetamol toxicity guideline 2021 (Rev 2, Dr M Tulleken, Dr TJ Gould) and the George EC two-bag NAC sheet (Oct 2026), checked against STG 2024 §19.5.1 (doses, nomogram Figure 19.1) and local facts rev 109 (L7 levels, M24 two-bag regimen, R5 liver unit, S13 psychiatry). Acute Handbook only. No international PDFs requested; the one point from ANZ 2020 (RSTI cut-off) is a DECISION.
