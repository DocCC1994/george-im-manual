Open questions for the section owner

  1. IV drugs stocked at George: which of adenosine, verapamil IV, digoxin IV, metoprolol or esmolol IV, amiodarone IV, lidocaine, isoprenaline and magnesium are in the EC and wards? STG lists no IV rate-control drug for fast AF, so the IV options on the acute page depend on this.
  2. Amiodarone IV regimen: STG 5 mg/kg over 30 min, or the ESC/RCUK 300 mg loading dose followed by 900 mg over 24 h? What concentration may run peripherally?
  3. Adenosine third dose: 12 mg (STG) or 18 mg (ESC, RCUK)?
  4. Cardioversion window: 24 h (ESC 2024) or 48 h (STG)? 3 weeks of anticoagulation (ESC) or 4 weeks of warfarin (STG)? Is TOE available anywhere we can reach?
  5. Stroke score: keep CHA2DS2-VASc (STG) or adopt CHA2DS2-VA (ESC 2024)?
  6. DOACs: is there a PTC route for a non-EML DOAC after failed warfarin (labile INR) or for patients who cannot reach INR monitoring? Any NEMLC update since December 2022?
  7. Warfarin start dose: STG 5 mg for everyone; should GRH use a lower start (2.5–3 mg) in the elderly, frail or those on interacting drugs?
  8. Heparin bridging: enoxaparin only when cardioversion is planned, or for every new AF started on warfarin?
  9. Sedation for cardioversion: midazolam by the medical team (STG), or ketamine or propofol with anaesthetics? Who may sedate in the EC?
  10. Pacing: which defibrillators can pace transcutaneously and where are they? Does anyone at GRH place transvenous wires? Which centre takes pacemaker referrals and how long is the wait?
  11. Mobitz I: follow STG (refer all second-degree block) or ESC (pace only if symptomatic)?
  12. Analgesia for pacing: STG says morphine 10–15 mg IM; prefer titrated IV morphine or fentanyl with midazolam?
  13. Atropine ampoules: 0.5 mg or 1 mg per mL locally? This decides whether to write 0.5 mg (RCUK) or 0.6 mg (STG).
  14. Defibrillator energies: confirm biphasic energies for the GRH devices.
  15. QT drug list: confirm the DR-TB and ART drugs listed and add any others commonly used at GRH.
