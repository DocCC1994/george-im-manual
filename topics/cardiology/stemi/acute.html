RECOGNISE

Ischaemic chest pain lasting ≥ 20 min, or an equivalent (dyspnoea, syncope, epigastric pain; may be silent in diabetes and the elderly), plus on ECG:

  • New ST elevation at the J-point in ≥ 2 contiguous leads: ≥ 1 mm in limb leads and V4–V6; in V2–V3 ≥ 2.5 mm (men < 40 y), ≥ 2 mm (men ≥ 40 y), ≥ 1.5 mm (women)
  • New or presumed new LBBB: apply Sgarbossa criteria
  • Inferior STEMI or ST depression V1–V3: record V4R and V7–V9

STEMI equivalents (left main pattern, de Winter, Wellens): no evidence for lysis, so discuss with GRH IM.

INVESTIGATE

12-lead ECG within 10 min of arrival; if not diagnostic, repeat every 20–30 min [STG 2024].

K+, creatinine, FBC, glucose, HIV; INR if on anticoagulants. Do not wait for troponin. CXR only if it does not delay lysis.

IMMEDIATE
  1. ABC, cardiac monitor, defibrillator at the bedside, 2 IV lines. Avoid arterial and central punctures.
  2. Oxygen only if SpO2 < 90% [ESC 2023]
  3. Antiplatelet (aspirin) 300 mg chewed now
  4. P2Y12 inhibitor (clopidogrel) 300 mg loading dose now; ≥ 75 y: 75 mg now (George and ESC, not STG)
  5. Fibrinolysis checklist + consent → non-fibrin-specific fibrinolytic (streptokinase) unless contraindicated; alternative: fibrin-specific fibrinolytic (alteplase) (dose box). Door-to-needle target ≤ 30 min.
  6. LMWH (enoxaparin) with fibrinolysis: IV bolus first if < 75 y (dose box).
  7. Pain: nitrate (ISDN) 5 mg SL, up to 3–4 doses after specialist discussion (not if SBP < 90, RV infarct or PDE-5 inhibitor); alternatively opioid (morphine) IV titrated, max 10 mg total.
  8. ECG 90 min after starting streptokinase (60 min after alteplase): < 50% fall in ST elevation in the worst lead = failed reperfusion → call GRH IM now.
ONGOING
  • High care, continuous monitoring ≥ 24 h; daily ECG.
  • Enoxaparin 1 mg/kg SC 12hrly until angiography or up to 8 days.
  • Aspirin 75 mg daily, lifelong.
  • Clopidogrel 75 mg daily: 1 month after lysis without a stent; 6 months if stented.
  • High-potency statin (rosuvastatin) 10 mg at night.
  • Once stable (no HF, hypotension, bradycardia, asthma): BB (atenolol) 50 mg daily.
  • HF or LVEF < 40%: ACEi (enalapril), titrate to 10 mg 12hrly; if not tolerated: ARB (losartan). (Echo before discharge, if possible.)
REFER

Discuss every STEMI with GRH IM. Urgent if: failed reperfusion · cardiogenic shock or pulmonary oedema · recurrent VT/VF · ongoing pain · new murmur (MR/VSD) · lysis contraindicated · new RBBB with fascicular block.
