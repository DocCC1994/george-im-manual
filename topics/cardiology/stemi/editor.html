Open questions for the section owner

  1. Which centre receives George STEMI referrals? Answered (local facts R1): GSH cardiology; letters typed and co-signed by the consultant. Transport goes in the introduction's transfer section.
  2. Will George adopt a routine pharmaco-invasive strategy (all lysed patients referred within 24 h), or rescue-only plus elective angiography? Local facts (R1): PCI rarely available; refer to GSH only after failed lysis per the Paarl post-STEMI PCI criteria. The pages now say pharmaco-invasive is not routine, pending this decision. Answered 20 Oct: ideal but usually not an option in RSA; rescue PCI via GRH IM, who discusses with GSH cardiology.
  3. Clopidogrel: give a 300 mg loading dose, and for how long (1 month per STG vs 12 months per ESC)? Answered 20 Oct: 300 mg load (75 mg if ≥ 75 y); 1 month after lysis without a stent, 6 months if stented.
  4. Aspirin loading 150 mg (STG) or 300 mg (GRH 2023)? Answered 20 Oct: 300 mg.
  5. Aspirin maintenance: Answered 21 Oct: 75 mg daily lifelong (GRH; STG 150 mg).
  6. Alteplase weight cut-off: 65 kg (STG 2024 text) or 67 kg (NDoH circular, GRH 2023)? Answered 20 Oct: 67 kg.
  7. Enoxaparin maintenance in patients ≥ 75 y: 0.75 mg/kg 12-hourly? Answered 20 Oct: keep 0.75 mg/kg 12hrly as written (local practice is often 1 mg/kg).
  8. Where is alteplase stocked in the EC, and who prepares it after hours? Answered 20 Oct: removed from the page.
  9. Local note on the RIC Africa study: the intro booklet says to discuss STEMI patients with the study team (R1). Add it to the local box?
  10. Paarl post-STEMI PCI referral criteria: should the page list them? The guideline is not yet in the project files.
  11. Streptokinase: how does George get it after the national discontinuation, and is supply reliable? Answered 20 Oct: removed from the page.
  12. Enoxaparin with streptokinase: STG says no heparin after SK; GRH 2023 gave enoxaparin with any lysis. Which? Answered 20 Oct: enoxaparin with any fibrinolytic, IV bolus if < 75 y.
  13. Streptokinase volume and rate: 200 mL over 60 min (GRH 2023) or 100 mL over 30–60 min (STG)? Re-use rule: never (GRH) or not within 5 days to 12 months (STG)? Answered 20 Oct: STG, 100 mL over 30–60 min; do not give if previous streptokinase.
  14. Is tenecteplase available through any route (EMS, private referral) that the page should mention?
