Definitions (STG 2024 §3.2.2; ESC 2023)

TermMeaning
NSTEMIIschaemic symptoms + troponin rise/fall with ≥ 1 value above the 99th centile, without persistent ST elevation. The ECG shows ST depression, T-wave inversion, or is normal.
Unstable anginaRest, new or crescendo angina (including post-infarct angina) with no troponin rise. It is less common with high-sensitivity assays.
Myocardial injury without ACSA raised troponin without ischaemic symptoms or ECG change. Look for heart failure, PE, CKD, sepsis, myopericarditis or aortic dissection.

Troponin

  • Use high-sensitivity troponin with the lab's validated 0 h/1 h or 0 h/2 h algorithm, which uses assay-specific cut-offs. If the result is ambiguous, repeat at 3–6 h [ESC 2023].
  • The GRH 2021 protocol (troponin I): onset < 6 h, sample at 0 h and 3 h and look for a rise or fall; onset > 6 h, > 500 rules in, < 18 rules out, and 18–500 needs a repeat at 3 h. The current assay is high-sensitivity troponin I: use the limits printed on the NHLS report rather than these numbers.
  • George: troponin at presentation and 6 h after maximum chest pain.
  • Troponin is never needed to start ACS treatment when the clinical picture is clear.

Scores: HEART for EC disposition of chest pain (GRH 2021) · TIMI (used in GRH 2021; named in the STG) · GRACE (named in the STG; ESC uses > 140 as a high-risk cut-off). For confirmed NSTE-ACS, use GRACE or TIMI (UA/NSTEMI).

Tertiary options

1. Immediate angiography for very-high-risk patients. Arrange it like a STEMI transfer.

2. Early or in-hospital angiography for high-risk patients, including everyone with a positive troponin [STG 2024].

3. CABG for left main or multivessel disease. Consent the patient for angiography and possible surgery before transfer.

4. Potent P2Y12 inhibitors and a factor Xa inhibitor (fondaparinux) are tertiary or ESC options; neither is on the STG regimen.

How to refer: GSH cardiology; typed letter co-signed by the treating consultant, saved in the IM discharge folder. Realistic wait for a non-urgent bed CHECK

Mitigation · while waiting, or if declined
  • Give full medical therapy: antiplatelet, P2Y12 inhibitor, anticoagulant, statin, BB, and ACEi if indicated.
  • Monitor in high care until pain-free ≥ 24 h; re-escalate at once if any very-high-risk feature appears.
  • Echo to assess LV function, regional wall motion and valves.
  • Look for and treat triggers: anaemia, hypoxia, arrhythmia, hypertension, thyrotoxicosis.
  • Low risk and not for transfer to GSH: stress ECG before discharge, booked in IM.
  • Document the discussion and the plan; book elective angiography if it was deferred.
  • Secondary prevention: stop smoking; control BP, diabetes and lipids.
