Open questions for the section owner

  1. Threshold: 180/110 (STG, ESC) or 180/120 (GRH 2021, AHA/ACC)?
  2. Terms: keep the STG three tiers (asymptomatic severe, urgency, emergency) or the ESC and AHA two (emergency, severe without acute organ damage)?
  3. Labetalol: bolus regimen (GRH), STG infusion (2 mg/min to 1–2 mg/kg), or both? Which concentration does pharmacy supply, and what maximum?
  4. Furosemide: routine (STG) or only when overloaded?
  5. ICH with SBP 150–220: leave alone (STG, GRH) or aim 140–160 (ESC 2024)? To be settled with the stroke topic.
  6. Stimulants: is labetalol acceptable after a benzodiazepine, or avoid all beta-blockers?
  7. Dissection: is IV metoprolol or esmolol stocked at George? Which centre takes type A dissections, and how fast is CT aortography after hours?
  8. Where: may labetalol and GTN infusions run in the EC and the medical high care, at what nursing ratio? Are arterial lines available outside ICU?
  9. No IV option: is oral amlodipine plus captopril acceptable as a bridge while transfer is arranged?
  10. Pregnancy: confirm the George obstetric protocol and threshold (160/110) so this page only points to it.
  11. Clinic target after discharge: < 140/90 (STG) or 120–129 systolic (ESC 2024)?
  12. Tests: which of LDH, aldosterone/renin, metanephrines and ADAMTS13 are available through NHLS George?

Verification note: STG 2024 §3.6.1–3.6.3 and Table 3.3 were read from the NDoH Chapter 3 PDF through a web reader (quotes on targets, labetalol, GTN, furosemide and enalapril). The GRH 2021 protocol (Rev 3) was read from the owner's Google Drive (George IM Handbook folder; the same text is in a PNG copy). The ESC 2024 hypertension guideline's Section 10 could not be read in full: only the summary recommendation tables were reachable, so the ICH (III B, IIa B), pregnancy (≥ 170/110) and “≥ 180/110 exclude an emergency” statements come from those tables and the Rev Esp Cardiol commentary (ICH 140–160). The condition table and IV drug doses come from the ESC Council 2019 position document, whose Table 4 has a published corrigendum for unit errors; doses were cross-checked with standard references but need a pharmacist check. The ESC 2024 aortic guideline and AHA/ACC 2025 citations were written from known references; click through once before sign-off. The SA maternity guideline was not read.
