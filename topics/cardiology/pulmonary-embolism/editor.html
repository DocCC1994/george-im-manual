Basis of this topic

There is no GRH clinical protocol for pulmonary embolism. The "PE GRH protocol" files in Drive are the PE_SA study protocol (HREC, version 3, 6 Oct 2026), which states that GRH and its drainage hospitals have no standardised PE protocol or decision rule; it was used for local context only. The topic is written from STG 2024 §2.8 and §20.12.4, then ESC/ERS 2019 (European, preferred), with AHA/ACC 2026 shown where it differs. The GRH warfarin protocol (2012) supplies the warfarin start. The Calgary Black Book (2022, p. 66) supplies the Wells two-level flowchart.

Open questions for the section owner

  1. Probability rule: Wells two-level (drafted; Calgary and ESC) or YEARS (fewer CTPAs)? Use the age-adjusted D-dimer cut-off? Which D-dimer assay does NHLS George run, in what units, and how long does it take?
  2. Fibrinolytic for high-risk PE: alteplase 100 mg over 2 hrs (ESC, AHA) or streptokinase 1.5 million IU over 2 hrs (cheaper, first-line for STEMI at George, but causes hypotension)? Where is it stocked?
  3. Intermediate–high risk: where are these patients monitored for 2–3 days (HOCU, ICU, ward)? Should any be discussed for transfer for catheter therapy (AHA 2b)? Which tertiary centre and how?
  4. Rivaroxaban: STG excludes age > 65 (insufficient evidence); ESC and AHA have no age limit. Keep the STG exclusion? Who may prescribe rivaroxaban for VTE at George, and is it available on discharge?
  5. Duration after unprovoked PE: STG says discuss with a specialist and limits rivaroxaban to 3 months; ESC (IIa) and AHA (class 1) extend. What is George's default?
  6. Active cancer: LMWH for 6 months (ESC) or warfarin (cost, injections)?
  7. Early discharge of low-risk PE: adopt (sPESI 0, normal RV, Hestia negative, follow-up booked)? Who sees them, and when?
  8. Patients on rifampicin: LMWH throughout, or warfarin with more frequent INR? Pharmacist to confirm ART interactions.
  9. Risk categories: keep ESC four tiers, or add the AHA/ACC 2026 A–E categories?
  10. UFH infusion dose and aPTT targets: is there a GRH heparin nomogram to cite instead?
