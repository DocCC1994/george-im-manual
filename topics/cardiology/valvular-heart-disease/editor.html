Open questions for the section owner

  1. Secondary prophylaxis duration: lifelong for all persistent rheumatic valve disease (STG), or by severity (WHO/AHA: mild RHD 10 years or to 21–25; moderate or severe to 40 or lifelong)?
  2. Benzathine benzylpenicillin interval: every 3–4 weeks (STG) or every 4 weeks, with 3-weekly only for high-risk patients (WHO)? Where are the monthly injections given (T9)?
  3. Mechanical valve INR target: keep the GRH 2.5–3.5 for all, or adopt the ESC targets by valve type and risk factors? The GRH Warfarin Protocol dates from 2012 and is due for review.
  4. Bridging: who manages warfarin and bridging for mechanical valves at George (T10), and which patients are bridged?
  5. Endocarditis prophylaxis in RHD: all RHD (STG) or high-risk only (ESC)? To be decided together with the Infective endocarditis topic.
  6. Referral triggers: print both the STG clinical triggers and the ESC echo triggers, or one set only?
  7. Rheumatic valve follow-up: is there a valve clinic or a GSH outreach list, and who decides on surgical referral (T9)? Answered: GSH cardiology first; medical management unless an acute surgical indication (R1, R2).
