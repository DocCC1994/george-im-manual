  2. Steroid in TB pericarditis: none in anyone (local teaching note), or prednisone for HIV-negative patients (ESC IIb C; IMPI showed less constriction)? If yes, which regimen?
  3. Diagnostic tap in suspected TB without tamponade: tap every moderate to large effusion for ADA and Xpert, or start TB treatment on the Tygerberg score and CXR alone?
  4. Empiric TB treatment threshold: Tygerberg score ≥ 6, a lymphocytic exudate, ADA ≥ 40 U/L, or a combination?
  5. Acute pericarditis: adopt the ESC regimen (ibuprofen 600 mg 8hrly + colchicine 3 months) although the STG has no pericarditis section? The STG uses ibuprofen 400 mg 8hrly elsewhere.
  6. Purulent pericarditis antibiotics: cloxacillin + ceftriaxone (draft), or vancomycin when MRSA is possible? SAASP 2026 has no section.
  7. Uraemic pericarditis: heparin-free dialysis as standard at George?
  8. Haemopericardium from dissection or rupture: keep "small-volume needle drainage only to keep a pulse" while GSH is called?
