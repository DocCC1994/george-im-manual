RECOGNISE

Sudden focal deficit: face droop, arm or leg weakness, speech, vision, balance. Time of onset = last seen well (waking with symptoms: when last awake and well) [GRH 2020]. Ask about anticoagulants.

Mimics: hypoglycaemia, seizure with Todd's paresis, migraine, sepsis or delirium, subdural haematoma, tumour, hypertensive encephalopathy, overdose. HIV: toxoplasmosis, TB, cryptococcus, PML, syphilis (needs contrast CT and LP).

Score the NIHSS (chart with the lysis checklist). Thunderclap headache or neck stiffness: subarachnoid haemorrhage.

INVESTIGATE

Bedside glucose now. Non-contrast CT brain within 30 min of arrival: excludes haemorrhage [GRH 2020]. FBC, U&E, INR and PTT (do not wait for them unless on an anticoagulant or a bleeding disorder is suspected), HIV, syphilis serology, lipids, ECG (AF, MI) [STG].

Age < 45: echo (RHD, cardiomyopathy, endocarditis), urine dipstick, ANF, RF, FBC; women: PTT and antiphospholipid antibodies; consider dissection [STG]. Carotid Doppler (anterior circulation, potential surgical candidate).

IMMEDIATE
  1. ABC: O2 only if SpO2 < 95% [GRH 2020]; protect the airway if GCS low. Glucose < 3.3: dextrose 50% 20 mL IV. No arterial or central line punctures [GRH 2020].
  2. Ischaemic stroke within 4.5 hrs with a disabling deficit and no exclusion: discuss with the IM consultant and thrombolyse [GRH 2020] [ESO] DECISION: STG says ≤ 3 hrs; GRH and ESO 4.5 hrs. Informed consent. Door-to-needle target 60 min [GRH 2012].
  3. Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg IV (maximum 90 mg): 10% as a bolus over 1 min, the rest over 60 min, in its own line [STG]; "stroke kit" from the emergency pharmacy CHECK stock and who prepares it after hours (M1).
  4. BP before lysis < 185/110 and below 180/105 for 24 hrs after: labetalol 10–20 mg IV, repeat once, then infusion [GRH 2020].
  5. Not for lysis, no bleed on CT: aspirin 300 mg orally or by NG tube now [STG]; after lysis wait 24 hrs and a repeat CT. Minor stroke (NIHSS < 4) or TIA with ABCD2 ≥ 4: add clopidogrel 300 mg once, then dual antiplatelets for 21 days [GRH 2020] DECISION: not in STG.
ONGOING

After lysis [GRH 2012]: BP and neuro obs every 15 min for 2 hrs, every 30 min to 6 hrs, then hourly to 24 hrs. Headache, vomiting, new deficit, GCS fall or BP surge: stop the infusion, urgent CT. No antiplatelets, anticoagulants, NG tube, catheter or arterial puncture for 24 hrs. Admit to ICU CHECK still GRH practice.

No lysis if CHECK list
  • Haemorrhage or large established infarct on CT; symptoms of SAH
  • Stroke or head injury < 3 months; ever intracranial bleed, tumour, AVM or aneurysm
  • BP > 185/110 despite treatment; glucose < 2.8
  • Platelets < 100; INR > 1.7; DOAC; heparin < 48 hrs with raised PTT
  • Active bleeding; arterial puncture at a non-compressible site < 7 days
  • Relative: surgery < 14 days, GI or urinary bleed < 21 days, MI < 3 months, seizure at onset, pregnancy, NIHSS > 25, rapidly improving non-disabling deficit [GRH 2020]
Local notes · George
  • Lysis done at George when patients arrive in time; no thrombectomy (GSH ~4 hrs by road) (R4).
  • After hours CT: non-contrast CT brain only (I1). No MRI (I4).
  • No stroke unit; family medicine manages most patients (S14).
BLEED

Intracerebral haemorrhage: stop and reverse anticoagulants (anticoagulant bleeding). BP: SBP > 180 or MAP > 130: labetalol IV to about 160/90 [GRH 2020]; AHA/ASA aims for SBP 140, avoiding drops below 130 [AHA/ASA] DECISION: BP target. Head up 30°. Seizures: status epilepticus.

Subarachnoid haemorrhage: thunderclap headache, neck stiffness, low GCS. CT; if normal, LP for xanthochromia (≥ 12 hrs after onset) [STG] CHECK timing. No aspirin, no NSAIDs. Paracetamol, then morphine IV to a total of 10 mg [STG]. Nimodipine 60 mg orally 4hrly for 21 days, in consultation with neurosurgery [STG].

Phone GSH neurosurgery (R4): SAH, cerebellar or posterior fossa bleed, hydrocephalus, falling GCS in a lobar bleed. Large bleed with deep coma: palliative approach.

BP

Ischaemic, not lysed: stop usual antihypertensives; do not lower BP below 220/120 in the first days [STG]. BP > 220/120: lower to about 180/110 over 24 hrs [STG]: STG uses CCB (amlodipine) 5 mg orally; GRH uses IV labetalol to lower by 15% [GRH 2020] DECISION. Treat sooner for a hypertensive emergency (dissection, MI, pulmonary oedema): hypertensive emergencies. Reintroduce antihypertensives cautiously after 7 days [STG].

ONGOING
  • Swallow screen before anything by mouth: alert, sitting up, 10 mL water; fails: nil by mouth, NG tube (not within 24 hrs of lysis), speech therapist [STG] [GRH 2020].
  • Sodium chloride 0.9% for the first 24–48 hrs while nil by mouth. Glucose 4–10: insulin if persistently > 10. Treat fever (paracetamol) and look for a cause.
  • VTE prophylaxis: immobile ischaemic stroke: LMWH (enoxaparin) 40 mg SC daily, not in a large infarct (> 1/3 MCA), haemorrhagic transformation or bleeding [GRH 2020] CHECK dose. No compression stockings.
  • Secondary prevention (ischaemic): aspirin 150 mg daily and a statin (rosuvastatin 10 mg at night; on amlodipine: simvastatin 10–20 mg) regardless of LDL [STG] DECISION: GRH 2020 simvastatin 40 mg.
  • AF or other cardioembolic source: warfarin (INR 2–3) from day 7–14, up to 21 days for large infarcts; no heparin bridging [STG]. GRH: as early as 48 hrs after a minor stroke [GRH 2020] DECISION: timing. No state DOAC for AF (M20).
  • Severe stroke or deteriorating: palliative care approach with the family [STG].
REFER

Call GRH IM for every patient within 4.5 hrs of onset, falling GCS, or a bleed. Rehabilitation: physiotherapy, OT, speech therapy, dietitian, social worker; consider Western Cape Rehabilitation Centre CHECK referral route.

TIA

ABCD2: age ≥ 60 (1) · BP ≥ 140/90 (1) · weakness (2) or speech only (1) · 10–59 min (1), ≥ 60 min (2) · diabetes (1). ≥ 4 = high risk: same-day workup [STG].

Mitigation · neurosurgery declines or delays
  • Head up, BP control, reverse anticoagulants, nimodipine for SAH; reassess GCS hourly.
  • Agree a ceiling of care with the IM consultant and family.
Tertiary options
  • GSH neurosurgery: SAH, aneurysm, posterior fossa bleed (R4).
  • Thrombectomy: not reachable in time from George (R4).
