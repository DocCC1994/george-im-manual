Basis of this draft

Acute batch 3 (22 Oct 2026): rough Acute-only draft, markers left open, no guideline PDFs requested. Tier 1 topic: the reference chapter is still owed (not part of this batch). Base: GRH stroke and TIA guideline (effective 01/01/2020, Dr T Gould) and GRH stroke thrombolysis protocol (2012), both on Drive; checked against STG 2024 §14.1 (stroke, TIA, SAH). ESO 2021 (thrombolysis) and AHA/ASA 2022 (intracerebral haemorrhage) were not read: points taken from them are marked DECISION. The 2012 protocol's "Tridal" (GTN) option and its age > 80 and glucose > 20 exclusions were left out because the 2020 guideline supersedes them.
